How it works

  1. Make a room, open its admin page, and type your Twitch, Kick, YouTube or Rumble channel.
  2. Edit the word list to fit your stream, and press Start game.
  3. Viewers type !enter in chat to get a card. You and your mods call words with !mark or on the admin page.
  4. Viewers search their name on your room page to watch their card fill in. Bingos and blackouts pay points, with weekly and all-time leaderboards.
Rooms that are not used for a few months are removed.
